## Everything else

The remaining 19 instrument banks are vendored from
[nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) at commit `622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5`, thinned from its
full sample set down to the roots this app actually loads (see
tools/fetch_instruments.mjs). Code there is MIT; the samples themselves are
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), sourced by that
project from Freesound, Versilian Studios (VSO2 / VSCO Community), Karoryfer
Samples, and the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios instrument
database — see that repository's own `sample-source-info.txt` for the
per-file breakdown.

Instruments included: bass-electric, bassoon, cello, clarinet, contrabass, flute, french-horn, guitar-acoustic, guitar-electric, guitar-nylon, harmonium, harp, organ, saxophone, trombone, trumpet, tuba, violin, xylophone.
